# PowerBI_Credit_Card_Customer_Report_Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Credit Card Customer Report Dashboard** is an interactive business
intelligence dashboard designed to analyze customer revenue, transaction
activity, income, demographics, and customer characteristics.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key credit card customer
metrics and helps users explore patterns across gender, age group,
salary group, dependent count, marital status, education level, customer
job, and state.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

-   Monitor overall credit card customer revenue and income.
-   Analyze transaction amount and customer spending patterns.
-   Compare revenue trends between male and female customers.
-   Understand customer distribution across different demographic
    groups.
-   Identify the top states contributing to revenue.
-   Analyze revenue and transaction behavior by customer job.
-   Provide interactive filters for deeper analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

-   **Power BI** -- Dashboard development and interactive visualization
-   **Power Query** -- Data cleaning and transformation
-   **DAX** -- KPI and analytical calculations
-   **Microsoft Excel / CSV** -- Data source and preliminary data
    preparation

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard displays the following major KPIs:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  KPI                                 Description
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Total Revenue**                   Total revenue generated from credit
                                      card customers

  **Total Interest**                  Total interest amount generated

  **Total Income**                    Combined customer income

  **Average CSS**                     Average customer score/value
                                      represented in the dashboard
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

### 1. Revenue Trend by Gender

A line chart showing monthly revenue trends for male and female
customers.

**Purpose:** - Compare revenue contribution by gender. - Identify
monthly revenue changes. - Observe peaks and declines throughout the
year.

### 2. Age Group Analysis

A stacked bar chart showing revenue/customer value across different age
groups and gender.

Age groups include: - 20--30 - 30--40 - 40--50 - 50--60 - 60+

### 3. Gender Analysis

A visual comparison of total contribution by gender.

The dashboard snapshot shows: - **Male:** 30.22M - **Female:** 25.09M

### 4. Customer Job Analysis

A table that compares customer job categories using:

-   Total Revenue
-   Total Transaction Amount
-   Total Income

Job categories include: - Blue-collar - Businessman - Govt - Retirees -
Self-employed - White-collar

### 5. Top 5 States by Revenue

A horizontal bar chart displaying the five states with the highest
revenue contribution.

The dashboard currently highlights: - Texas (TX) - New York (NY) -
California (CA) - Florida (FL) - New Jersey (NJ)

### 6. Salary Group Analysis

A gender-wise comparison of revenue/value across:

-   Low
-   Mid
-   High

This helps understand how customer salary groups contribute to the
overall business.

### 7. Dependent Count Analysis

A stacked bar chart comparing customer contribution by the number of
dependents and gender.

### 8. Marital Status Analysis

A comparison across: - Married - Single - Unknown

The visual is segmented by gender to identify differences in customer
contribution.

### 9. Education Level Analysis

A gender-wise analysis of customers across education categories:

-   Graduate
-   High School
-   Unknown
-   Uneducated
-   Post-Graduate
-   Doctorate

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

-   **Quarter**
-   **Week Start Date**
-   **Gender**
-   **Card Category**

These filters update the KPIs and visualizations based on the selected
values.

## 📋 Dashboard Layout

The dashboard is organized into four major sections:

**Top Section** - Dashboard title - Quarter filter - Week Start Date
filter - Gender filter - Card Category filter

**KPI Section** - Total Revenue - Total Interest - Total Income -
Average CSS

**Analysis Section** - Revenue Trend by Gender - Age Group - Gender
contribution - Customer Job Analysis

**Customer Demographic Section** - Top 5 States by Revenue - Salary
Group - Dependent Count - Marital Status - Education Level
